Firefox Add-on listing copy (paste into addons.mozilla.org)

Name
De-slopify

Summary
Remove Gemini buttons and AI Overviews from Google Search, Gmail, Docs, Drive, and YouTube.

Description
De-slopify hides Google’s AI chrome so Search, Gmail, Docs, Drive, and YouTube stay the product — not Gemini.

It removes:
- AI Overviews and the AI Mode tab on Google Search (desktop and mobile)
- Ask Gemini, Help me write, summarize, and Refine in Gmail, Docs, Drive, Calendar, and Meet
- Gemini chips and AI summaries on YouTube

It does not change gemini.google.com. That site is the Gemini app.

Use the toolbar popup to turn the add-on ON or OFF, or to disable Search, Workspace, or YouTube separately.

Privacy
De-slopify does not collect or transmit data. Settings stay on your device. Full policy: https://github.com/HeavenlyCatCodes/De-slopify-Google/blob/main/PRIVACY.md
